# Study Techniques That Work

## Pomodoro Technique
Work in focused blocks of 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. After four blocks, take a longer break of 15 to 30 minutes. This reduces burnout and makes starting easier because the commitment is small.

## Spaced Repetition
Review material at increasing intervals: 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, then 21 days. Flashcard apps like Anki automate the schedule. Spaced review beats cramming for long-term retention.

## Active Recall
Close your notes and try to write down or explain what you remember. Testing yourself is far more effective than re-reading. Use practice questions and past papers.

## Exam Week Plan
Two weeks before: finish the syllabus once. One week before: solve past papers under timed conditions. Last two days: review weak topics only and sleep at least 7 hours.
