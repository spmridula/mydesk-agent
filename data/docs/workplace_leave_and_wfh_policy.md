# Workplace Leave, WFH and Expenses Policy (Sample)

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees get 18 days of paid annual leave per year, plus 8 days of sick leave. Unused annual leave up to 10 days carries forward to the next year. Apply for leave in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance for leave longer than 2 days.

## Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00, when you must be available on chat. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from the department head.

## Expense Claims
Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense with original receipts. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 1,200 rupees per day. Claims above 10,000 rupees need approval from a director.

## Probation
The probation period is 3 months. Leave during probation accrues but sick leave only can be taken in the first 3 months.
