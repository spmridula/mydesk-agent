# IT Help Desk FAQ (Sample)

## Resetting the VPN
Open the VPN client, click Settings, then Reset Profile. Sign in again with your single sign-on credentials. If you see error 809, restart your router and try again, and if it persists, raise a ticket with IT.

## Forgot Password
Go to the company or college login page and click "Forgot password". A reset link is sent to your registered email and expires in 30 minutes. Passwords must be at least 12 characters.

## Wi-Fi Not Connecting
Forget the network and reconnect. Make sure you pick the secure network, not the guest network. Devices must be registered at the IT desk before first use.

## Software Installation
Install only approved software from the Software Center. For anything else, raise a request with business justification and manager approval.
