# University Exam and Assignment Policy (Sample)

## Late Submission
Assignments submitted up to 24 hours late lose 10% of the marks. Submissions between 24 and 72 hours late lose 25%. After 72 hours, the submission is not accepted unless an extension was approved.

## Extensions
Request an extension at least 48 hours before the deadline through the student portal. Medical or family emergencies need supporting documents such as a doctor's note. Each course allows a maximum of one extension of up to 5 days.

## Re-evaluation of Exam Papers
Students can apply for re-evaluation within 10 working days of results being published. The fee is 500 rupees per paper. The marks after re-evaluation are final.

## Attendance
A minimum of 75% attendance is required to sit for end-semester exams. Students between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation with a valid reason.
