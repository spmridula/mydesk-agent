# Interview and Career Prep Guide

## STAR Method
Answer behavioral questions using Situation, Task, Action, Result. Describe the context briefly, state your responsibility, explain the specific steps you took, and finish with a measurable result.

## Resume Tips
Keep it to one page for under 5 years of experience. Start bullets with strong action verbs and include numbers, such as "reduced API latency by 35%". Tailor the skills section to each job description.

## Technical Interview Prep
Practice data structures and algorithms for 45 to 60 minutes daily. For system design, learn load balancing, caching, databases, and queues. Explain your thinking out loud while solving problems.

## Salary Negotiation
Research the market range before the call. Let the employer share a number first when possible. Negotiate the whole package: base pay, bonus, joining bonus, and learning budget. Always stay polite and enthusiastic.

## Follow-Up
Send a thank-you email within 24 hours of the interview, mentioning one specific topic from the discussion.
